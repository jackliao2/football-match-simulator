# Three national-team URL reviews — 28 October 2026

Reviewed the historical record, full editorial dossier, playable players, starting XI, formation, summary, SEO title/description, achievement claims and the shared landing evidence for these routes. This was a factual review, not just a source-link insertion. Numerical player ratings were retained as model estimates; no ratings were invented for missing historical players.

Implementation: `data/reviewed-national-records.ts` exports `REVIEWED_NATIONAL_RECORDS`; the parent change integrates it into `SEARCH_PRIORITY_RECORDS`. That table supplies the visible historical facts and route-specific FAQs. The three dossiers in `data/team-editorial-nations.ts` now cite sources and distinguish the historical registrations from playable selections.
